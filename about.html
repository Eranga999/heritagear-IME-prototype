Our story

From a research lab at SLIIT to the foot of Lion Rock.

HeritageAR began as a final-year research project at the SLIIT Faculty of Computing. We built and tested a working prototype at Sigiriya — and are now turning it into Sri Lanka's offline digital heritage platform.

Vision

“To become the leading offline digital heritage platform that makes cultural heritage exploration interactive, accessible and informative for every visitor.”

Mission

“To use mobile AI, computer vision and augmented reality to deliver accurate, engaging and affordable heritage experiences that work without continuous internet connectivity.”

Our values

What guides every site pack we build.

Accuracy & respect for heritage

All content is validated against reliable sources and presented respectfully.

Accessibility for all

Offline use, many languages and voice interaction widen access.

Privacy by design

Camera frames and questions never leave the visitor's phone.

Innovation with purpose

AI and AR solve real visitor problems — not novelty.

Complementing, not replacing

We support human guides, heritage staff and educators.
