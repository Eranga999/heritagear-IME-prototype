Design thinking

How we designed HeritageAR.

  1. 01

    Empathise

    Studied visitors at Sigiriya and other sites: weak signal, short information boards, language barriers and guides that aren't always available.

  2. 02

    Define

    Visitors need accurate, multilingual heritage guidance and in-site directions that work without the internet.

  3. 03

    Ideate

    Combined four functions — recognition, AR overlay, an offline assistant and visual navigation — into one on-device app.

  4. 04

    Prototype

    Built a working Flutter prototype at Sigiriya with a YOLOv8n INT8 model, offline RAG and step-and-compass navigation.

  5. 05

    Test

    User validation with at least 100 tourists and students before launch; feedback improves models, content and routes.
